POKEMON CHARACTERS AND ANIMATIONS
Models: Bulbasaur (001), Charmander (004), Squirtle (007), Pikachu (025).
Source repository: https://github.com/06wj/pokemon

Source paths: public/models/{001,004,007,025}/model.glb
Retrieved: 2026-10-22. Delivered GLBs are unmodified source files.
The six embedded skeletal clips are attack, happy, idle, run, sleep and walk.
Runtime additions: scale normalization, independent animation mixers, upper-body
blending, procedural knockback/falling, water cannons and refill tanks.
There is no dedicated imported hit, swimming or falling animation.

GARDEN ENVIRONMENT AND WATER EFFECTS
assets/environment-v3.glb contains project-authored Blender geometry/materials.
water-flow.js uses emitted water packets, detached droplets, ripples and shader
fountains. These are visual approximations, with separate gameplay collision.
Water cannons and tanks are project-authored procedural Three.js props.

AUDIO
Fountain Days: project-composed and synthesized island music, 108 BPM.
No borrowed game music or external samples. Runtime effects use Web Audio.
Source: game-audio.js, source/audio/create_water_party_music.py.
