Water Push v0.4 — asset provenance and modifications

CURRENT CHARACTER GEOMETRY
assets/character-v3.glb
Original WaterCrew mesh authored for this project in Blender: rounded head/body,
facial features, cap, clothing and shoes. The current export contains only the
new WaterCrewV3_Character mesh (12 material primitives), not the old Soldier mesh.
Runtime tinting provides four identities. Water gun and backpack are separate
original runtime props attached/positioned by Three.js.

RETAINED SKELETON AND ANIMATION
Creator: Quaternius
Pack: Toon Shooter Game Kit
Official source: https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original source file: Character_Soldier.gltf
Downloaded from the official Google Drive glTF folder on 2026-10-04.
The v0.3 original geometry uses the retained 43-joint skeleton and 17 source clips:
Death, Duck, HitReact, Idle, Idle_Shoot, Jump, Jump_Idle, Jump_Land, No, Punch,
Run, Run_Gun, Run_Shoot, Walk, Walk_Shoot, Wave, Yes.
No dedicated strafe, backward, refill, swimming or falling-into-water struggle
clips were added. Runtime blending, aim adjustment and falling translation are
code-driven additions. Death is the source death/fall-over clip, not a custom
swimming animation. CC0 provenance applies to the retained rig and clips; it is
not a claim that Quaternius authored the new WaterCrew mesh or the garden scene.

LEGACY SOURCE FOR REPRODUCTION
assets/character.glb and assets/source/Character_WaterCrew.gltf are the v0.2
adaptation of Quaternius Character_Soldier. Original firearm/weapon meshes,
shoulder armour and chest pouches were removed; the source face/skin geometry,
bones and all 17 clips were retained. These legacy files remain as rig/action
inputs and provenance material. The current build does not embed them as its character.
Blender editable character scenes may retain these old source meshes hidden for
reference; only the newly authored character mesh and rig are exported to v0.3 GLB.

GARDEN ENVIRONMENT
assets/environment-v3.glb
Original project-authored Blender mesh geometry and materials: bevelled stone
platform, leaves, flowers, pool, lawn, pedestal/fish-spout forms and nozzle markers.
Dynamic fountain streams, foam, surface effects and waterfalls are rendered by
Three.js. The reference video informed visual and gameplay direction; no Nintendo
models, animation, music or sound were extracted or included.

ORIGINAL v0.4 WATER VFX MODULE
water-flow.js is original project code shared by the playable scene, decorative
fish spouts and character viewer. It uses world-space emitted water packets,
a tube reconstructed from live packets, detached ballistic droplets, impact
spray, horizontal-plane ripple instances, and GPU ballistic fountain particles
with a time-gated aerated rise. These are game visual-effects approximations,
not a fluid solver. They do not solve pressure, fluid volume conservation,
inter-droplet interactions or arbitrary scene collision. Gameplay collision and
knockback remain separate from the visible water simulation. Character/environment
geometry, skeleton and source animation provenance above are unchanged in v0.4.

RUNTIME AND OTHER MATERIAL

reflection helpers: MIT. See vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt and preserved source headers.

concept.png is the project's earlier generated concept illustration, retained as
historical exploration; it is not a screenshot of the v0.3 playable scene.
UI, gameplay code, authored geometry scripts, runtime props/effects and temporary
synthesized sounds were created for this prototype. No additional open-source
license is asserted here for original project material.
